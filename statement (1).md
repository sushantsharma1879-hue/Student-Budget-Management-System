
# Project Statement

## Problem Statement

Students often find it difficult to keep track of their daily spending and monthly budgets. This project provides a simple system to record expenses and monitor spending.

## Objective

The objective is to develop a Python application that allows students to manage expenses, set budgets, and generate spending reports.

## Target Users

College and university students.

## Main Modules

1. Expense Management
2. Budget Management
3. Reports and Analytics

## Expected Outcome

The system helps students record expenses, monitor category-wise budgets, and understand their spending patterns.
